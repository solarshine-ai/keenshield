How it works

Three seconds of attention, only when it matters.

  1. 01

    Browse like normal

    Keenshield sits quietly on the shield icon. No banners, no interruptions, nothing blocked — ever.

  2. 02

    Glance at the number

    Every page gets a score from 1 to 10. Most sites stay low. It moves when a page asks you to agree, pay, subscribe or hand over something personal.

  3. 03

    Click to read why

    One click shows the reasons in plain English — the auto-renewal, the off-platform deposit, the lookalike address. Then you choose.

1–3Normal. Nothing worth flagging.
4–6Legitimate, but read before you agree.
7–10Predatory terms or likely scam.

What to watch for

    Trust & privacy

    A shield you can see through.

    An extension that reads web pages has to earn your trust. Here is exactly what Keenshield does with what it sees, in plain words.

    It never blocks or interrupts
    Keenshield only shows a number on its icon. It doesn't stop pages from loading, cover them with warnings, or pop anything up.
    Ordinary pages: only the address is sent
    On a page that doesn't ask you to agree, pay, subscribe or share personal details, the extension sends the web address only — checked for things like lookalike domains — and the page's text goes nowhere.
    We read the company's pages, not yours
    To score the company behind a site, Keenshield's own server visits that company's public pages — its homepage, terms, privacy, refund, cancellation and pricing pages — the way anyone could. It never sees what you saw, and nothing about you is stored with the score.
    Decision pages: text is analyzed, then discarded
    On checkout, sign-up, terms and similar pages, the visible text is sent to Keenshield's server and analyzed by Anthropic's Claude AI. We don't save the text. We keep the score and reasons for that page's address — without anything after a "?" — so the next person gets the answer instantly.
    Your data doesn't train AI
    Anthropic does not use data sent through its commercial API to train its models by default.
    Photos are cleaned first
    Photos you scan are resized in your browser before upload, which strips hidden location data. They're analyzed and not kept.
    No ads. No selling your browsing.
    Keenshield has no ads and never sells or shares what you browse. There are no affiliate deals, and no business can pay to change its score.
    What we do keep
    A random install or browser ID to count free scans (or a count on your account if you sign in), a short-lived count of requests per network address to stop abuse, and your email only if you join the list or send us a message below.
    Not legal advice
    Keenshield points out what's worth a second look. It can miss things, and it isn't a lawyer.
